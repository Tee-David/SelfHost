# Code of Conduct

SelfHost contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technical and constructive.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate in reviews, critique ideas and code rather than people, provide enough context for others to participate, respect privacy and security, and assume good faith while still holding changes to a high engineering standard.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly introducing malicious or deceptive changes are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or other participation that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or project.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately rather than opening a public issue.
